Who I am

I bring education leadership, corporate leadership, project management and digital innovation together. I have guided teachers, learners, schools, foundations and technology-focused programs, and I turn digital tools and emerging technology into practical classroom and community solutions.

I advocate for girls' rights, safe schools and youth empowerment through Shule Salama and related initiatives, promoting protective, inclusive, confidence-building learning environments. I mentor girls with disabilities to take part confidently, hold visible roles and feel equally valued alongside their peers.

Day to day, I coordinate education and technology programs, support teachers in using interactive digital tools, and promote digital entrepreneurship for children. Classroom expertise, strategic leadership, partnership engagement and practical delivery all come with me.
